How this mock works

  • 1One question is shown at a time, exactly like a real computer-based mock test. Every question has 5 options — click one to answer.
  • 2The moment you answer, the full step-by-step solution appears right below the question, along with the correct option and whether you got it right.
  • 3The exam timer starts when you begin. It shows the total time you took, so you know in how many minutes you completed the full exam. A per-question timer also runs — and it stops the moment you choose your answer for that question.
  • 4Everything auto-saves in your browser's local storage. Close the tab anytime — when you reopen this file, you resume exactly where you left off (answers, position and timer included).
  • 5Use the question palette on the right to jump to any question. Green = correct, red = wrong, white = not answered. You can change an answer anytime before submitting.
  • 6When you finish, press Submit Test to see your score, accuracy, time analysis and full solution review.
